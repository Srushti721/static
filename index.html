About

These living spaces are built to look like the natural homes of the animals. Zookeepers feed the animals, give them clean water, and provide medical care when they are sick.
People of all ages love to visit the zoo to see wildlife up close. Visitors can watch majestic lions resting, playful monkeys swinging, and colorful peacocks dancing. Walking through the park helps everyone learn new facts about nature and understand why we must protect endangered species. Overall, a trip to the zoo is both a fun adventure and a great learning experience.
